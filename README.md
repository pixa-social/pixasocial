## Psychographics first

Age, city, and job title are a starting point. They are not a strategy.

PixaSocial is built on the world’s best psychological models for campaigns. You describe the person you need to move — what they want, what they fear, what they already believe — and the studio turns that into a living persona. Copy, images, and UGC videos are then aimed at *that* psyche.

**Methods stay proprietary. Results stay yours.**

| Instead of… | PixaSocial models… |
| --- | --- |
| “Women 25–34, USA” | The buyer who says yes because the message confirms who she already believes she is |
| A generic brand voice | A persona with goals, fears, and a decision style |
| One caption for everyone | Operators — campaign mechanics that attach a feeling to your brand |
| Stock product footage | Brand Influencer & Product UGC videos spoken to that psychographic |

### How the model becomes work

1. **Personas** — psychographics, beliefs, goals, and fears. Not a demographic sketch.
2. **Operators** — the campaign mechanic. What feeling should attach to the brand, and what cue carries it.
3. **Content** — captions, stills, PowerPoint, and short video written for that mind.
4. **Brand Influencers & Product UGC** — sit-down and handheld videos that look like a real creator, aimed at the same psychographic.
5. **Publish** — plan the week, approve, then post to the channels you connect.

PixaSocial is powered by science-grade insight into personality, motivation, and decision-making — turned into personas, operators, and content that win.
