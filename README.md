## The studio

### Create
- **AI Chat** — write posts, generate images, build a PowerPoint, summarize a PDF or Word file, research the web, and save media to your library
- **Personas & operators** — audience models and campaign operators that stay on-brief
- **Creative tools** — social images, logo studio, ecommerce editor, multi-image edit
- **Voice & video** — voice generation on Essentials+; promo video on Studio (video packs anytime in Billing)

### Plan & publish
- **Content planner** and **calendar** — see the week, click a day, work the queue
- **Channels** — connect X, Instagram, LinkedIn, Reddit, and more. YouTube and TikTok are coming soon
- **Media Studio** — one library for generated stills, imported images, documents, and saved Reels / X videos

### Grow
- **Precision targeting** — reach the right people at the right time
- **Analytics** — see what landed
- **Desktop-first studio** — full creative tools on a computer; mobile stays useful for signup, captions, and posting
